Context

Following Q2 revenue miss, three of five churned customers cited pricing confusion as a primary driver. The current feature-gated model creates unequal value delivery — small teams pay the same as large ones for features they rarely use.

This document captures the live state of the Option B evaluation: a pure usage-based credits model. It is updated automatically from Slack, meetings, and Jira as new signals arrive.
