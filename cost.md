# Stratis Monetization Plan

Stratis can make money as a B2B SaaS product for leadership, product, strategy, and operations teams that need better decision tracking across meetings, documents, and internal tools.

## 1. Core Business Model

Use a subscription model priced per workspace and per active strategic project. This fits the product because the value is tied to team coordination, decision velocity, and the number of high-stakes initiatives being tracked.

### Recommended Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Target Customer | Price | Includes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Starter | Small leadership teams | $49/user/month | Strategy map, meeting capture, decisions panel, basic integrations |
| Team | Product and GTM teams | $99/user/month | Advanced signals inbox, live transcript linking, document generation, Slack/Notion/Jira integrations |
| Business | Mid-market companies | $199/user/month | AI decision scoring, risk prediction, admin controls, export, workspace analytics |
| Enterprise | Large organizations | Custom | SSO, audit logs, custom retention, dedicated support, private deployment options |

## 2. Usage-Based Revenue

Add usage pricing for expensive AI and transcription features.

| Usage Item | Billing Model |
|---|---|
| Live meeting transcription | Included allowance, then per recorded hour |
| AI decision analysis | Included monthly credits, then per extra AI credit |
| Historical pattern search | Per advanced research query |
| PDF/document export | Included for paid tiers, bulk export as enterprise feature |
| Extra integrations | Included in higher tiers or sold as add-ons |

Example: Team plan includes 100 AI credits and 50 transcription hours per month. Extra transcription could cost $1.50 to $3.00 per hour depending on provider cost and margin target.

## 3. High-Value Add-Ons

These can increase average contract value without making the base product too expensive.

| Add-On | Why Customers Pay |
|---|---|
| Stratis Pebble device | Dedicated meeting capture hardware for exec rooms |
| Advanced AI risk forecasting | Helps leadership identify stalled or risky decisions earlier |
| Compliance and audit pack | Needed by finance, healthcare, legal, and enterprise customers |
| Executive reporting pack | Auto-generated weekly strategy brief for leadership |
| Custom integrations | Connect internal tools, CRMs, BI systems, or private knowledge bases |

## 4. Hardware Revenue

The Pebble device can be sold separately or bundled with higher plans.

Possible pricing:

| Option | Price |
|---|---:|
| One-time device sale | $149 to $299 per device |
| Hardware rental | $15 to $25 per device/month |
| Enterprise room bundle | Custom contract |

The device should not be the main profit center early. It should help differentiate the product and reduce meeting capture friction.

## 5. Best Initial Customer Segments

Start with customers where poor decision tracking creates visible business cost.

1. Product organizations managing roadmap tradeoffs.
2. SaaS leadership teams making pricing, packaging, and GTM decisions.
3. Strategy and operations teams coordinating cross-functional initiatives.
4. Consulting firms that need to turn client meetings into decision records.
5. Enterprise transformation teams managing complex internal programs.

## 6. Sales Motion

Use a hybrid motion:

| Motion | Fit |
|---|---|
| Self-serve trial | Small teams testing the strategy map and meeting capture |
| Product-led expansion | Teams invite stakeholders into decision reviews |
| Sales-assisted | Mid-market customers needing integrations and security review |
| Enterprise sales | Large organizations needing SSO, audit logs, retention, and procurement |

## 7. Free Trial Strategy

Offer a 14-day trial with:

- 1 workspace
- 2 active projects
- 5 recorded meetings
- Limited AI credits
- Slack and Notion integration

The trial should show the full workflow: meeting capture, decision extraction, strategy map updates, and final strategy brief.

## 8. Key Value Metrics

Pricing should connect to measurable customer value.

| Metric | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Active strategic projects | More projects means more value and complexity |
| Recorded meeting hours | Drives transcription cost and product usage |
| AI analysis credits | Directly maps to LLM cost |
| Number of integrations | More connected sources increase stickiness |
| Decision records created | Shows whether Stratis is becoming system of record |

## 9. Revenue Expansion Paths

After the first team adopts Stratis, expand by:

- Adding more departments to the workspace.
- Charging for more active projects.
- Selling executive reporting to leadership.
- Adding paid integrations for enterprise systems.
- Bundling Pebble devices for meeting rooms.
- Offering annual contracts with onboarding support.

## 10. Simple Go-To-Market Offer

Initial paid offer:

**Stratis Team Plan**

- $99/user/month
- Minimum 5 users
- 50 transcription hours/month
- 100 AI credits/month
- Slack, Notion, Google Calendar, and Jira integrations
- Living strategy document export

This creates a starting contract value of about **$495/month** or **$5,940/year** per team before usage overages and add-ons.

## 11. Main Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI and transcription costs reduce margins | Add included usage limits and overage pricing |
| Customers see it as a note-taking tool | Position around decision intelligence, not meeting notes |
| Hardware slows adoption | Make Pebble optional and support browser/cloud meeting capture |
| Enterprise sales cycle is slow | Start with product and strategy teams before full enterprise rollout |
| Integrations are expensive to maintain | Prioritize Slack, Notion, Google Calendar, and Jira first |

## 12. Best Monetization Path

The best path is:

1. Launch as SaaS first.
2. Charge per user with usage limits.
3. Add AI and transcription overages.
4. Use Pebble hardware as a premium differentiator.
5. Move larger customers into annual Business and Enterprise contracts.
